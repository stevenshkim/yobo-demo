**Owner:** Steven (founder) · **Market:** United States · **Last updated:** 2026-06-03

> Built on the 5 "dangerously good at content" videos (Modern Millie, Colin & Samir, Kallaway, Wes McDowell, Charlie Morgan). The single most important borrowed principle for us: **we are Wes McDowell / Charlie Morgan, not MrBeast.** Polished videos get views. Raw, helpful videos get clients. Our scoreboard is booked demos and closed MRR — not subscribers.

## 0. The one-line content hypothesis

> **"We'll be different and more valuable because we show US Shopify founders the exact Klaviyo flows, emails, and numbers that an AI growth team ships — in public, with real revenue, every week."**

If a piece of content doesn't make a Shopify operator think *"that's literally my store / my problem,"* we don't make it. (Kallaway's rule: no differentiated hypothesis = no reason to follow.)

## 2. Reach vs. Revenue — pick the scoreboard first

This is the Wes McDowell / Charlie Morgan fork, and we are deliberately on the **revenue** side.

- **We do NOT optimize for virality.** A 2,000-view video that books 8 qualified Shopify founders beats a 200,000-view video that books zero. (Charlie Morgan books 400+ calls/mo off a ~30K-sub niche channel — that's the model.)
- Every asset is **top-of-funnel for a demo of Aria**, not a vanity metric.
- North-star content metric: **Qualified demos booked per 1,000 views (QD/1K).** Optimize that number, not raw reach.

## 4. Content pillars (the idea machine)

Five repeatable pillars so we never create from a blank page (Modern Millie's topic→idea→calendar system):

1. **Teardowns** — Roast/rebuild a real (or anonymized) Shopify brand's flows. *"I audited a $2M skincare brand's Klaviyo — here's the $40K they're missing."*
2. **Build-in-public** — Aria shipped X this week, here's the revenue. Transparent dashboards, real numbers. (This is our Charlie Morgan "build in public" engine.)
3. **How-to / templates** — The exact flow, the exact subject lines, the exact segment. Give the recipe away. (Wes McDowell: be genuinely, boringly helpful.)
4. **Contrarian POV** — *"Stop A/B testing button colors,"* *"Your welcome series is too long,"* *"Why we fired the 'spray and pray' agency model."* Earns the click + the share.
5. **Proof / case studies** — Brand went from $X to $Y. Named when possible, anonymized + screenshot when not. Dollars front and center.

**Mix per 10 pieces:** 3 teardowns · 2 build-in-public · 3 how-to · 1 contrarian · 1 proof.

## 5. Packaging system (the part most people skip)

> Colin & Samir + Kallaway: **the title + thumbnail IS the product.** Make it first. If it doesn't earn the click, kill the idea before you film.

**Title formulas (US founder-tuned):**
- Specific-dollar + mechanism — *"This Klaviyo Flow Made a Shopify Brand $11,400 in 9 Days"*
- Contrarian + stakes — *"Your Abandoned Cart Email Is Costing You $8K/Month"*
- Curiosity + identity — *"I Let an AI Run a Shopify Store's Email for 30 Days (Nobody Does This)"*
- Recipe — *"The 5-Email Win-Back Flow Every Shopify Brand Should Steal"*

**Thumbnail rules:**
- **One idea, big.** A dollar number, a before/after, or a single screenshot — readable at phone size.
- High contrast, real faces/real Klaviyo UI > stock. Emotion + clarity over polish.
- Never explain in the thumbnail what the title already says — they must *combine*, not repeat.

**Packaging gate:** every idea gets a title + thumbnail sketch in the backlog *before* it's greenlit. No package, no production.

## 6. The content → client funnel

Content is the top of a system, not the end of it. (Charlie Morgan: content pre-qualifies buyers.)

```
Helpful content (YT/LinkedIn)
   → "Want the actual template/flow?" (free lead magnet)
   → Email newsletter nurture (we eat our own cooking)
   → Soft CTA: "See Aria run your store free for 2 weeks" (the /start trial in the demo)
   → Booked demo / trial
   → Close ($499 / $1,499 / Custom)
```

- **Every video/post ends with one CTA** — the 2-week trial (mirrors the `/start` onboarding already in the product) or "book a teardown."
- **Lead magnets that match each pillar:** flow templates, a "Klaviyo revenue audit" checklist, swipe-file of subject lines. Gated by email → fuels the newsletter.
- **Retargeting:** anyone who watches >50% or downloads a magnet gets retargeted with a proof/case-study asset.

## 7. Production cadence & system

Sustainable beats heroic (Modern Millie). Volume + iteration is how the break-out hit arrives (Kallaway hit it on videos 5–6).

| Asset | Cadence | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube long-form (8–15 min) | 1× / week | Main rock |
| YouTube Shorts / vertical clips | 3–4× / week | Cut from long-form |
| LinkedIn post (text/carousel) | 4–5× / week | Build-in-public + repurpose |
| X posts/threads | daily | Operator notes, fast |
| Newsletter | 1× / week | The owned asset |

**Weekly operating rhythm**
- **Mon:** pull last week's real Aria results → that's the build-in-public + proof content.
- **Tue:** package next 2 ideas (title + thumbnail), greenlight.
- **Wed:** film the flagship long-form.
- **Thu:** edit + cut 3–4 verticals.
- **Fri:** schedule everything for the following week; newsletter goes out.

**Quality bar (Kallaway):** perceived quality drives trust on a small channel — get **audio clean** first, then framing. Don't over-produce; do sound right.

## 9. Metrics & review

**Weekly scoreboard (in priority order):**
1. **Qualified demos booked** (the only one that matters)
2. **QD per 1,000 views** (efficiency)
3. Newsletter subscribers + open rate
4. Watch-time / retention (leading indicator of packaging quality)
5. CTR on thumbnails (packaging health)
6. Raw views (lagging, lowest priority)

**Monthly:** double down on the top 2 performers by QD, kill the bottom 2 formats, re-package and re-test the "near misses" (good retention, weak CTR = fix the title/thumbnail and re-shoot the hook).

## 10. 90-day rollout

- **Days 1–30 — Foundation.** Lock positioning + 5 pillars. Ship 4 flagship videos (ideas 1–4). Stand up the newsletter + first lead magnet (win-back template). Daily LinkedIn build-in-public. Goal: *system running, first demos trickling.*
- **Days 31–60 — Volume + iteration.** 4 more flagships, full Shorts/clip pipeline live, 2 lead magnets. Start retargeting watchers. Identify the first break-out format. Goal: *find what converts, QD/1K climbing.*
- **Days 61–90 — Double down.** Pour resources into the winning format. Land 1–2 founder podcast guest spots (borrow US ecom audiences). First named case-study video. Goal: *predictable demos/week from content.*
