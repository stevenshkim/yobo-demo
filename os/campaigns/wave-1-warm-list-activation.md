# Wave 1 — Warm-List Activation Sequence

**Funnel:** 1 — Lead Generation → 2 — Activation (the hook is a free Growth Audit that *is* the trial's first step).
**Segment:** our existing warm audience — list subscribers, social followers, waitlist, past demo/contact — US Shopify + Klaviyo DTC founders & growth leads.
**Channel:** Email (4 touches) + LinkedIn (2 touches, in parallel for connected founders).
**Goal:** claim a **free Growth Audit** → connect Shopify + Klaviyo → product-led trial → first paid customers. The cheapest ~30 customers toward **$100k MRR**, at ~$0 CAC.
**Voice:** Aria (`os/company/brand-voice.md`). Warm — they already know us, so lead with a *gift*, not a pitch.

> **The offer that runs through everything: the free Growth Audit.** Aria reads their
> connected Shopify + Klaviyo and returns a credible, brand-specific read of how much repeat
> revenue their email is leaving on the table — plus the plan to capture it. It's the lead
> magnet, the activation "aha," and the BOFU anchor all at once
> (see `bofu-pricing-and-conversion.md`).
