## Hero
**Headline:** You're leaving money in your email — let's prove it, free.
**Sub:** Connect your Shopify + Klaviyo (5 min, read-only). Aria shows you exactly how much repeat
revenue your email is leaving on the table — and the plan to capture it.
**CTA button:** Run my free Growth Audit →
**Trust line:** Read-only access · no card · no call.

## What you get (the audit deliverable)
- **Your email revenue gap, in dollars** — what you're leaving on the table per month.
- **The 3 flows most likely leaking money** right now (Win-Back, Abandoned Cart, Welcome…).
- **A real plan** built from your store data — not a generic template.

## How it works (3 steps)
1. **Connect** Shopify + Klaviyo (5 minutes, read-only).
2. **Get your audit + plan** — built from your own numbers.
3. **See it work** — start a free trial, Aria ships your first flow live, you watch the revenue. Then decide.
