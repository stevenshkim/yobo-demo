# Wave 2 — Cold Outbound Sequences (by ICP segment)

**Funnel:** 1 — Lead Generation (channel C2). **Goal:** ~150 trials → ~45 customers toward
**$100k MRR**, CAC ~$150. **Channel:** Email (4 touches) + LinkedIn (2), per segment.
**Load with:** `os/company/icp.md`, `offer-and-positioning.md`, `brand-voice.md`.

> **Cold rule:** lead with a *specific, true observation about THEIR store* — never "I help
> brands…". The signal *is* the opener. CTA every time = the free **Growth Audit**.

## ⚠️ Before sending — dependencies
- **Deliverability (build plan A4) is NOT yet set up.** Do not send cold volume until secondary
  domains + warming + SPF/DKIM/DMARC are live, or you'll torch the company domain.
- **Compliance (CAN-SPAM/CASL):** real sender identity, valid physical address, working
  one-click unsubscribe, honest subject lines. Honor opt-outs immediately.
- **Personalize the `[signal]`** per prospect from public data (Meta Ad Library, their site,
  Klaviyo footprint, reviews). Generic = spam; specific = replies.

## Signals to open on (per the 9-dimension ICP)
Stale/empty Klaviyo flows · running ads (Meta Ad Library) to a leaky funnel · high review count
at 4+/5 · replenishable/high-frequency product · recently raised · seasonal moment · community.
