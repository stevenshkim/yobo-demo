# Sales-Assist Playbook (warm + high-value accounts)

**Funnel:** 1→2. **Job:** convert the accounts the self-serve motion shouldn't handle alone —
**stalled high-intent trials** and **$5–20M Head-of-Growth buyers** (Senior / Head of Growth tiers,
where higher ACV absorbs the human cost). Default motion stays product-led; this is the assist.
**Voice:** Aria/founder. **Load with:** `icp.md`, `bofu-pricing-and-conversion.md`, `guarantee-mechanics.md`.

> Sales-assist is *results-first too* — you're not "selling," you're walking a high-fit buyer
> through their own audit + a live result. The product does the convincing.

## When to trigger sales-assist
- An **ICP-fit trial stalls** after connecting (activation nudge N6 didn't recover it).
- A **$5–20M brand** starts a trial or requests a demo (higher ACV → worth the human touch).
- A **warm/partner-referred** high-value lead comes in.
- Inbound from the founder content (Tier-1) asking to talk.

## The flow (keep it tight)
1. **Discovery (10 min):** their store, their goal, what's broken in email today.
2. **Show the audit:** their dollar gap, live, from their data (`growth-audit-builder`).
3. **Show a live result or plan:** the first flow shipped (or ready to ship) — proof, not promise.
4. **Frame the offer:** the right tier as the *hire they're making*; anchor on salary.
5. **Close on the guarantee:** "3× your fees back or you don't pay." Low-risk yes.

## Discovery questions
- "What's your current repeat-purchase rate / how often do customers reorder?"
- "What flows do you have live in Klaviyo today? When did you last touch them?"
- "What are you spending on acquisition vs. retention?"
- "If email drove an extra $[X]/mo, what would that change for you?"
- "Who needs to say yes besides you?"

## The pitch (results-first, Aria voice)
> "Here's your audit: you're leaving ~$[gap]/yr in email. I've already built your [Win-Back] —
> it's [live / ready]. At [tier], Aria runs your whole lifecycle for $[price]/mo — a fraction of a
> [senior growth marketer at $130–175k]. And it's guaranteed: 3× your fees back in revenue within
> 90 days, or you don't pay until it hits. Want me to turn the rest on?"

## Objection handling (from BOFU — sharpened for live conversation)
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "It's a lot per month." | "It's ~1/10th of the senior hire doing this — and you've seen the gap. The guarantee means it pays for itself or you don't pay." |
| "We have an agency." | "Run us free alongside for 90 days. Compare the attributed revenue. Keep whoever wins." |
| "Need to think / loop in my team." | "Totally — your audit + first flow stay live while you decide. Want me to ship one more so there's more to judge?" |
| "How is this different from a tool?" | "A tool needs someone to run it. Aria *is* the someone. You approve; she does the work." |

## The close
- Tier recommendation (default **Senior $1,499** for $5–20M; **Head of Growth $2,999** for multi-store / max scope).
- Restate the guarantee + "keep the flows." Send the order link on the call.
- If not now: agree the next flow Aria ships + a date to review results. Never leave without a next step.

## What to log back (CRM + `os/funnels/02-activation.md`)
Demo→paid % by segment · which objections recur · which tier high-value accounts land on
(feeds blended ACV) · stalled-trial→saved rate · time from demo to close.
