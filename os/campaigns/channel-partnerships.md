# Channel Partnerships Engine (channel C6)

**Funnel:** 1 — Lead Generation. **Target:** ~60 trials → ~18 customers toward **$100k MRR**,
**CAC ~$50**, compounding. The lowest-CAC, highest-trust channel — other people's audiences
vouching for us.
**Load with:** `os/company/icp.md`, `offer-and-positioning.md`, `proof-library.md`.

> Partnerships are slow to start and compounding once live. Begin in Wave 2, expect the payoff in
> Wave 3 and beyond (post-July this becomes a primary, near-$0-CAC engine).

## Partner types & the play for each

| Partner type | Why they refer | The play | Incentive |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Klaviyo ecosystem** | we make Klaviyo succeed → stickier for them | join Klaviyo's partner/agency program; get listed; co-marketing; reps referring brands that need help | partner status, co-marketing |
| **Shopify ecosystem** | complementary, not competitive | Shopify Plus partner network; agencies; app-adjacent placements | referral fee / co-marketing |
| **Non-email agencies** (web, design, paid, SEO) | they don't do email; clients ask for it | "send your clients' email/retention to us, keep them happy" | **rev-share (10–20% recurring)** |
| **Creators / influencers** (DTC/ecom) | their audience = our ICP | sponsored teardowns, affiliate links to the free audit | affiliate / flat sponsorship |
| **Communities & newsletters** (DTC Slack/Discord, masterminds, ecom newsletters) | members want growth | sponsorship, AMAs, free-audit offers for members | sponsorship / rev-share |
| **Complementary apps** (reviews, SMS, subscriptions, loyalty) | shared customer, non-overlapping | co-marketing, integration, cross-referral | mutual referral |

## The referral program (customers + partners)
- **Structure:** recurring **rev-share (10–20%)** for partners; for customers, a **bounty or
  account credit** per referred paying brand. *(Confirm % + terms with Steven/Legal.)*
- **Attribution:** unique referral links/codes → CRM (A1). Track partner-sourced audits → trials → paid.
- **Make it easy:** one-click referral link, a swipe file (the partner one-pager + email copy below),
  and a dashboard of their referrals + earnings.
- **Fuel it with proof:** every happy customer (`proof-library.md`) → testimonial + referral ask
  (handoff from Customer Success).

## The partner one-pager (the core asset — Aria/brand voice)
A single page a partner can forward:
1. **Who we are:** the AI-native growth marketer for consumer brands — we run Klaviyo end-to-end.
2. **Why refer us:** your clients/audience want repeat revenue; we deliver it, guaranteed (3× fees back).
3. **What's in it for you:** [rev-share %] recurring / [bounty]; we make you look good.
4. **What we do for them:** free Growth Audit → trial → results-first → guaranteed.
5. **How to refer:** your link + a ready-to-send intro email.

## Partner outreach (to land partners — short, value-first)
- **Agencies:** "You don't do email; your clients keep asking. Refer it to us, keep them happy, earn [%] recurring. Free audit for any client, no risk to you."
- **Creators:** "Your audience is exactly who we help. Want to do a teardown together / affiliate the free audit?"
- **Communities:** "Can I offer your members a free Growth Audit + a teardown session? Pure value, no pitch."

## Measurement
- Partners signed · partner-sourced audits → trials → paid · partner CAC (~$50 target) · rev-share paid vs MRR generated.
