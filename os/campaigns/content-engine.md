# Content Engine — Blueprint + Starter Scripts

**Funnel:** 1 — Lead Generation (channels C3 content + C4 posting/engagement in `os/gtm-build-plan.md`).
**Job:** the compounding, lowest-CAC demand channel. High-volume content across a network of
accounts → drives free **Growth Audit** claims at trending-toward-$0 CAC.
**Load with:** `os/company/icp.md` (the lanes), `brand-voice.md` (Tier-1), `offer-and-positioning.md`.

> **One idea → many formats → many accounts → one CTA (the free Growth Audit).** Volume is the
> point, but volume of *on-lane, culturally-resonant* content — generic content doesn't spread.

---

## 1. The two-tier account architecture (decided)

| | Tier 1 — Brand + Founder | Tier 2 — AI educational accounts / AI influencers |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | YoboLabs brand · Steven (founder POV) | a network of AI creators, one per lane |
| **Posture** | **Play it safe** — educational + *light* promotional | **Heavy lifting** — aggressive promotional, "what helps businesses grow" |
| **Voice** | Aria voice (`brand-voice.md`) / Steven's real voice | each account's own persona (not Aria) |
| **Risk** | brand-protective — never risk a ban | diversified + expendable; a ban on one ≠ company risk |
| **Platforms** | LinkedIn (founder), IG, YouTube | TikTok, IG Reels, YouTube Shorts, X, Facebook |
| **Goal** | trust, category authority, inbound | reach, virality, audit clicks at scale |

**Brand-safety rule:** the company never bets survival on a bannable tactic. Tier 1 stays
squeaky-clean; Tier 2 takes the risk, spread across many accounts and platforms.

## 2. The lane network (community × category) — spin up in this order

Lanes come straight from the ICP virality lens (`os/company/icp.md`). Each lane = an AI
educational account/influencer producing content in the community's **language + culture**,
for a **category** where lifecycle email prints money. Start with the highest success-score ×
virality combos:

| Priority | Lane | Language | Platform(s) | Persona concept | Pillar focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **DTC email teardowns (general)** | English | TikTok, Shorts, X | "the email growth nerd" | Teardown + education |
| 2 | **Latina beauty** | Spanish + Spanglish | TikTok, IG Reels | a Latina beauty-growth creator | Teardown + community proof |
| 3 | **Korean / K-beauty** | English + Korean | IG, YouTube | K-beauty growth creator | Education + before/after |
| 4 | **Indian F&B / supplements** | English + Hindi | YouTube, IG | desi DTC growth creator | Replenishment education |
| 5 | **African-American haircare/beauty** | English | TikTok, IG | Black-owned-brand growth creator | Community proof + teardown |
| 6 | **Founder POV (Tier 1)** | English | LinkedIn, X | Steven, real | Build-in-public + POV |

> Expand to more clusters (Brazilian, Middle Eastern, European, Chinese, etc.) once the first
> lanes prove a content→audit→trial path. **Don't boil the ocean — prove one lane, then clone.**

## 3. The 5 content pillars (mix target)

| Pillar | % of output | What it is | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Teardowns** | 35% | "I audited a [category] brand's email — here's the $ they're leaving on the table." | reach + the audit CTA |
| **2. Before/after revenue** | 20% | a real flow shipped → real revenue (from Proof Library) | proof + conversion |
| **3. Education / how-to** | 25% | "The 3 flows every Shopify store needs," tips | authority + saves/shares |
| **4. Founder POV / build-in-public** | 10% | Steven's POV, the mission, the numbers (Tier 1) | trust + inbound |
| **5. Social proof** | 10% | testimonials, results, "why they switched" | conversion |

> Teardowns are the workhorse — they show expertise, are infinitely repeatable across lanes,
> and lead naturally to "want me to do this for *your* store? → free Growth Audit."

## 4. The production line (idea → published, at volume)

```
IDEA (lane + pillar)
  → SCRIPT (OS writes — hook + beats + on-screen text + CTA)
  → CREATIVE (image/video-gen for AI-influencer face/B-roll; Canva for graphics)
  → CAPTION + HOOK variants (OS — 3 hooks per piece)
  → SCHEDULE (social scheduler, per cadence)
  → ENGAGE (comment/DM loop → warm responders hand off to outreach)
  → LOG (reach, audit clicks, by lane/pillar/hook → back into this file + lead-gen playbook)
```

**Repurposing matrix — 1 idea becomes ~6 assets:** one teardown → a 30s TikTok/Reel/Short
(×3 platforms) + a LinkedIn text post (Tier 1) + an X thread + a carousel. Reshoot the same
script per lane with the matched persona/language.

## 5. Hook library (first 3 seconds — the whole game)

- "I audited a $[X]M [category] brand's email. They're losing $[gap] a year." 
- "Your Shopify store is leaving money in your email and you don't even know it."
- "This [category] brand has 50,000 customers and emails them… never."
- "3 Klaviyo flows every store needs. Most have zero." 
- "A growth marketer costs $8–11k/month. Here's how to get the work for $499."
- "Stop running ads to a leaky bucket. Fix this first."
- (Spanish) "Tu tienda está perdiendo dinero en tus emails — y ni lo sabes."

> Always test 3 hooks per piece. Hook = ~80% of performance. Log winners.

## 7. Posting cadence & engagement loop (C4)

- **Cadence:** TikTok/Reels/Shorts daily per active Tier-2 lane; LinkedIn 3–5×/wk (Tier 1); X daily.
- **Engagement loop:** comment on ICP founders' posts, reply to every comment, DM warm responders
  → hand to outreach (`cold-outbound-writer`). Light-touch + human-supervised (no bulk automation).
- **Tooling:** needs a social scheduler + light editing; no native MCP posting (see build plan).

## 8. Brand-safety & compliance guardrails (non-negotiable)

- **Label AI content** where platforms require it (TikTok/Meta AI-content labels) — disclosed AI
  is *more* ToS-safe than hidden automation, and protects the lanes.
- **FTC:** AI-influencer accounts promoting YoboLabs must disclose the material connection
  (#ad / "from the team at YoboLabs") — they're our accounts, not independent reviewers.
- **No fabricated results.** Every number traces to `os/company/proof-library.md` or is marked
  `[PLACEHOLDER]`. No real-looking stats until they're real.
- **Tier 1 stays clean.** Never run risky automation on the brand/founder accounts.

## 9. KPIs & what to log back (to `os/funnels/01-lead-generation.md`)

- Per lane/pillar/hook: views, watch-through, audit-page clicks, audits claimed, → trials → CAC.
- Winning hooks + formats (promote them; clone winning lanes).
- Which **community lanes** convert best — feeds ICP priority + which lanes to scale next.

> A piece of content that doesn't end in an audit click is awareness, not acquisition. Always
> tie the CTA to the free Growth Audit, and log the click-through by lane.
