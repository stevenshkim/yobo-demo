# Activation — Onboarding & Drop-off Nudges

**Funnel:** 2 — Activation. **Job:** get more trials to the "aha" (connect both → credible plan →
approve first flow → see it ship), faster. Recover drop-offs at each step.
**Primary metric:** trial→paid % (≥ 30%). **Voice:** Aria. **Load with:** `os/funnels/02-activation.md`.

> Each nudge maps to a specific drop-off step. Short, outcome-led, one CTA. Plain-text-friendly so
> the same content works in-product, email, and (later) WhatsApp.

## The path & the nudge at each step
```
Trial start → Connect Shopify → Connect Klaviyo → First plan → Approve flow (AHA) → Ships → Results → Paid
```

### N1 — Started but didn't connect (within ~1h, then +1 day)
> **You're one step from your Growth Audit.**
> Connect Shopify + Klaviyo (5 min, read-only) and I'll show you exactly how much repeat revenue
> your email is leaving on the table. **[Connect & see my audit →]**

### N2 — Connected Shopify only (missing Klaviyo)
> **Almost there — I just need Klaviyo too.**
> Shopify shows me your customers; Klaviyo lets me see (and fix) your flows. Connect it and your
> audit's ready in minutes. **[Connect Klaviyo →]**

### N3 — Plan delivered, not yet approved a flow (the key drop-off)
> **Your plan's ready — here's the fastest win.**
> Your [Win-Back] flow is built and ready to ship. It targets [N] lapsed customers — real revenue,
> on your brand, you approve before anything sends. **[Review & approve →]**

### N4 — Approved, flow is live (reinforce the win)
> **Your [Win-Back] flow is live in Klaviyo.** 🎉
> I'll report the revenue as it comes in. Want me to queue your [Abandoned Cart] next? **[Yes, build it →]**

### N5 — First results in (pre-paywall value)
> **First numbers from your flow: $[X].**
> This is live in your store right now. There's more in your plan — [next flows]. **[See the plan →]**

### N6 — Stalled high-intent (ICP-fit) → sales-assist trigger
> *(Human, you/team)* "Saw your [Win-Back] is performing — anything blocking you from keeping it
> going? Happy to walk through your numbers." *(Cheapest conversion you'll get.)*

## Then → conversion (BOFU)
At trial-end the post-trial sequence takes over (`os/campaigns/bofu-pricing-and-conversion.md`) —
results-first, price reveal as "keep Aria running."

which first flow produces the fastest visible win · stalled→saved rate from sales-assist.
