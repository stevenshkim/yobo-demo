# Customer Success — Weekly Recap + At-Risk Saves (templates)

**Funnel:** 3 — Customer Success. **Job:** keep churn low (retention protects CAC) and make value
*felt* weekly. The weekly recap is the single most important retention artifact.
**Voice:** Aria (`brand-voice.md`). **Load with:** `os/funnels/03-customer-success.md`, `proof-library.md`.

> Retention protects the $100k: a churned customer shrinks MRR *and* poisons the referrals/proof
> the cheap channels run on. Silent months kill renewals — never go quiet.

## 1. Weekly revenue recap (the hero-number template)

> **[Brand] — this week with Aria** 📈
>
> **$[X,XXX] in email revenue this week.**
>
> Three things that moved:
> • Win-Back flow recovered [N] customers → $[X]
> • Abandoned Cart is now 3 emails → +[X]% recovered
> • [Campaign] sent to [segment] → $[X]
>
> You're at **[Y]× your fees** toward the 3× guarantee. *(7-day Shopify-matched — `guarantee-mechanics.md`)*
>
> Next week: [the one flow/campaign I'm shipping]. Approve and I'll build it.
> **[See the dashboard →]**

- Send weekly, same day. Always lead with the hero number. Plain-text-friendly (renders on any channel).

## 2. At-risk save playbook

**Trigger an outreach when a health signal goes red** (`os/funnels/03-customer-success.md` health model):
flows stalled · revenue trend down 2+ wks · ghosting approvals · "is this working?" · sends paused.

**Save message (proactive, before they complain):**
> **[First name] — quick check-in.**
> I noticed [specific signal: your sends paused / approvals stalled]. I don't want you leaving
> revenue on the table.
> Here's what I'd ship this week to get [Brand] back on track: [specific plan].
> Want me to build it? **[Yes, go →]**

- For high-value/at-risk: a human (you/team) follows up. Cheapest retention you'll get.

## 3. 30 / 60 / 90 health review (the renewal is decided in month 1)
- **Day 30:** confirm ≥ 3 flows live + a visible win. Recap the month's revenue + 3× progress.
- **Day 60:** trend check; propose the next expansion if healthy (`expansion-upsell-engine.md`).
- **Day 90:** the guarantee checkpoint; show the multiple; renew the relationship narrative.

## 4. Testimonial + referral ask (fires after a clear win)
> **[First name] — [Brand] just hit $[X] in email revenue with Aria.** 🎉
> Two quick asks: (1) mind if I write this up as a short result *(with your ok)*? (2) know another
> brand leaving money in their email? I'll give them the same free Growth Audit — and you'll get
> [referral incentive].

- Every captured win → `os/company/proof-library.md` (feeds content + the cheapest lead-gen channel).
