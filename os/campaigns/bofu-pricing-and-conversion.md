# BOFU Pricing & Conversion — Results-First

**Funnel:** 2 — Activation (trial → paid) + the pricing surface itself.
**Job:** convert a trial into paid at ≥ 30% by **selling the proven outcome before the price.**
**Load with:** `os/company/offer-and-positioning.md`, `north-star.md`, `brand-voice.md`.

> **The thesis in one line:** never sell the price — sell *keeping the teammate who's already
> making you money.* By the time pricing appears, the brand has a free audit quantifying their
> gap and a live flow producing revenue. Pricing is the easy "yes" at the end, not the pitch.

Each stage produces the asset the next one sells with. The **audit's dollar gap** and the
**trial's real revenue** are the two numbers that make price feel trivial.

## The pricing — growth-marketer tiers (LOCKED naming)

Frame Aria as **the growth-marketing hire you're making**, at the seniority your brand needs.
Price like software; anchor against the salary.

| Tier | Price | The hire it replaces | Loaded cost of that hire* |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Growth Marketer** | $499/mo | A mid-level growth marketer running your email | ~$6–8k/mo |
| **Senior Growth Marketer** | $1,499/mo | A senior who runs more flows + channels, faster | ~$10–13k/mo |
| **Head of Growth** | $2,999/mo | A growth leader across channels + multi-store | ~$15–20k+/mo |

## How to frame the pricing page (good-better-best)

1. **Lead with outcome, not tiers.** Top of page = the proven result + the audit's dollar gap,
   not a price grid. "You're leaving ~$[gap]/yr in email. Here's the plan to capture it."
2. **Three tiers, anchor high.** Show all three with **Senior Growth Marketer** as the visually
   recommended/default (most brands land here once they see value). Head of Growth at $2,999
   makes Senior look like the obvious, reasonable choice (price anchoring).
3. **Price each against its salary equivalent**, not against competitors. The comparison isn't
   "cheaper than another tool" — it's "1/15th the cost of the hire, working today."
4. **ROI math from *their* audit.** Auto-fill the page with their gap:
   "Senior is $1,499/mo = $18k/yr. Your audit found $[gap]/yr in recoverable email revenue.
   That's a [gap÷18k]× return before she touches a new channel."
5. **Risk reversal, front and center:**
   - Free audit + trial: *see real results before you pay.*
   - 3× guarantee (once Legal-cleared — `offer-and-positioning.md`): the mechanics, plainly stated.
   - "Keep the flows" — even if you cancel, the work she shipped to your Klaviyo is yours.
6. **One CTA, framed as continuation:** **"Keep Aria running"** — not "Subscribe" / "Buy."
   The mental model is retaining a producing teammate, not starting a bill.

## The price-reveal moment (the single most important message)

Don't reveal price until a flow is live and a result is in. Then, in the Aria voice:

> **Your Win-Back flow made $890 this week.**
> That's live in your Klaviyo right now.
>
> To keep me building — Win-Back, Abandoned Cart, Welcome, and the rest of your plan — it's
> **$499/mo.** A growth marketer doing this costs $6–8k and you'd manage them.
>
> Your audit found **$[gap]/yr** sitting in your email. Keeping me pays for itself in [X] weeks.
>
> **[Keep Aria running →]**

This reframes conversion from "should I start paying?" to "should I switch off something
that's already making me money?" — a much easier yes.

## Post-trial conversion sequence (if they haven't converted)

Fires when the trial window nears its end and a flow has shipped. Aria voice, results-led.

**Touch 1 — Day (trial end − 3): the scoreboard.**
> Subject: what I made you this week
> Body: lead with the live revenue number + the flows shipped. "Here's what's running and what it earned. To keep it going, it's $499/mo." CTA: Keep Aria running.

**Touch 2 — Day (trial end − 1): the gap + the math.**
> Subject: the $[gap] still on the table
> Body: their audit gap vs. what's been captured so far. "We've captured $[x]. There's $[gap] left. I've got the plan — want me to keep building?" CTA: Keep Aria running.

**Touch 3 — Day (trial end): keep your work.**
> Subject: your flows pause tomorrow
> Body: honest + calm. "Your trial ends tomorrow. The flows I built stay in your Klaviyo, but I stop improving and adding new ones. Keep me on for $499 and I'll ship [next flow] this week." CTA: Keep Aria running.

**Touch 4 — Day (trial end + 3): sales-assist trigger for ICP-fit stalls.**
> For high-fit accounts that stalled, a human (you/team) reaches out: "Saw your Win-Back is doing well — anything blocking you from keeping it going?" Cheapest conversion you'll get.

## BOFU objection handling

| Objection | Reframe (results-first) |
|---|---|
| "It's more than I expected." | "It's 1/15th of the hire doing this work — and you've already seen the revenue. What would a $6k/mo marketer have to make you to be worth it? Aria already did." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Totally fair. Your flows stay live while you decide. Want me to keep one more building this week so you've got more to judge by?" |
| "Can I start on the cheapest tier?" | "Yes — start as Growth Marketer at $499. Upgrade to Senior when you want SMS or the full plan. You're never locked in." |
| "How do I know it'll keep working?" | "You've already got week-one revenue and the 3× guarantee. If it doesn't deliver, you don't keep paying." |
