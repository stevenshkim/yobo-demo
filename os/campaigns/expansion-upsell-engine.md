# Revenue Expansion / Upsell Engine (templates)

**Funnel:** 4 — Revenue Expansion. **Job:** NRR > 100% and **lift blended ACV** — a direct lever
on $100k MRR (higher ACV = fewer logos needed). Every expansion dollar costs ~$0 CAC.
**Voice:** Aria. **Load with:** `os/funnels/04-revenue-expansion.md`, `offer-and-positioning.md`.

> Expansion is **earned by results** — you upsell from proven revenue, never on a calendar blast.

## The trigger map (which signal fires which offer)

| Trigger | Offer | Tier move |
|---|---|---|
| Hitting volume/flow limits on current tier | upgrade for more flows/cadence | Growth Marketer → Senior |
| List + revenue growing, email working | add SMS / WhatsApp channel | Senior → Head of Growth |
| Approving everything, wants more | full plan / more channels | → Head of Growth |
| Runs multiple Shopify stores | multi-store | Head of Growth |
| Proven happy at 3×+ | annual commitment offer | (cash + retention) |

## Upsell proposal template (proof → projected lift → one-click)

> **[First name] — email is producing $[X]/mo for [Brand].** 📈
> You're hitting the ceiling of what we can do on [current tier].
> If we add [SMS / more flows / next channel], I project **~$[Y]/mo more** based on your numbers.
> Same deal: you approve, I build, it's guaranteed.
> Move to **[Senior Growth Marketer / Head of Growth]**? **[Yes, upgrade →]**

- Trigger-based, one-click (same approval pattern as flows). Low friction = high attach.

## Cross-sell — new-capability announcement (when we ship SMS, analytics, etc.)
> **New: Aria now runs [SMS].**
> Your email is doing $[X]/mo. Adding SMS to your [Win-Back / launches] typically adds ~$[Y].
> Want me to build it into your plan? **[Add it →]**

- Send only to fitting accounts (per ICP), with an expected-revenue estimate from their data.

## Annual / commitment offer (for proven-happy customers)
> **You've hit [Y]× with Aria.** Lock in a year at [discount] and I'll prioritize [Brand]'s roadmap.

- Improves cash + retention. Offer after a clear, sustained win.

## Measurement
NRR by cohort · expansion MRR by path (tier vs channel vs capability) · attach rate per capability ·
which triggers convert best · **blended ACV trend** (feeds the `north-star.md` ~$750 assumption).

## What to log back (to `os/funnels/04-revenue-expansion.md`)
Which triggers convert to expansion · attach rate of each capability · expansion CAC (~$0 — quantify
the leverage) · how much expansion is lifting blended ACV toward/above the $750 working assumption.
