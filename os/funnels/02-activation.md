# Funnel 2 — Activation  ⭐ highest-leverage funnel

**Owns:** trial → "aha" moment → paid conversion.
**Primary metric:** trial→paid conversion % (target ≥ 30%).
**Why it's #1:** in a product-led motion, raising trial→paid from 20%→40% *halves* the
top-of-funnel (and CAC) needed for 200. This is where 200 is won or lost.

## The activation thesis

A trial converts when the brand reaches the **"aha" moment** — the instant they believe
"this will actually grow my revenue, on my brand, with little effort from me." For YoboLabs
that moment is concrete:

> **AHA = Connected Shopify + Klaviyo → saw a credible, brand-accurate growth plan built
> from *their own* store data → approved their first flow and saw it ship to Klaviyo.**

Everything in activation exists to get more trials to that moment, faster.

## The activation path (instrument every step)

```
Trial start
  → Connect Shopify            (drop-off risk: friction/trust)
  → Connect Klaviyo            (drop-off risk: friction/permissions)
  → Receive first plan         (must feel credible + on-brand, fast)
  → Approve first flow         ← AHA
  → Flow ships to Klaviyo      (proof it's real)
  → First results / recap      (reinforces value before paywall)
  → Convert to paid
```

| Step | Target completion | Biggest risk |
|---|---|---|
| Connect both Shopify+Klaviyo within 24h | ≥ 70% of trials | friction, trust, permissions |
| Receive first plan | ≥ 95% of connected | plan quality/speed |
| Approve ≥ 1 flow | ≥ 65% (the AHA rate) | plan not credible / not on-brand |
| Convert to paid | ≥ 30% of all trials | value not yet felt before paywall |

## Plays to raise trial→paid

1. **Reduce time-to-first-plan.** The faster a brand sees a credible plan from their own
   data, the higher the conversion. Make connection dead-simple; pre-fill from store data.
2. **Make the first plan undeniable + on-brand.** It must read like a senior strategist who
   knows *their* store. Use real Shopify/Klaviyo signals. Aria voice (`brand-voice.md`).
3. **Engineer the first win fast.** Push one high-confidence flow (Abandoned Cart / Win-Back)
   that produces visible revenue inside the trial window.
4. **Human assist for stalled high-intent trials.** Sales-assist (you/team) reaches out when
   an ICP-fit trial connects but stalls before approving. Cheapest conversion you'll get.
5. **Onboarding nudges in the Aria voice** at each drop-off step (connect reminder, "your
   plan's ready," "approve to ship," "here's week-1 revenue").
6. **Remove the paywall surprise.** Make the trial→paid moment feel like keeping a teammate
   who's already producing, not starting a subscription.
