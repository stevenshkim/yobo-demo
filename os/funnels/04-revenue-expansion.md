# Funnel 4 — Revenue Expansion

**Owns:** paid customer → more revenue per customer.
**Primary metric:** Net Revenue Retention (NRR) > 100% — the base grows even before new logos.
**Why it matters to the 200:** every dollar of expansion is a dollar you didn't pay CAC for.
Expansion is the highest-margin growth and the foundation for life after July. (Note: the
200 target is *net-new logos* at ≥ $500 — expansion doesn't count toward the logo count,
but it makes the whole model durable and funds CAC.)

## The thesis

YoboLabs lands with email (the wedge) and expands into the full "AI growth marketer." The
more of a brand's growth Aria runs, the higher the account value and the stickier it gets.
Expansion is *earned by results* — you upsell from a position of proven revenue.

## Expansion paths (in rough sequence of readiness)

1. **Tier upgrade** ($499 → $1,499): more flows, higher cadence, more volume. Trigger:
   customer hitting volume limits or asking for more.
2. **More channels:** add SMS / WhatsApp to email. Trigger: email working, list engaged.
   (WhatsApp not yet wired — `os/integrations/map.md`.)
3. **More of the growth job:** retention analytics, upsell/cross-sell flows, then paid
   acquisition assist — the roadmap toward "analyze everything, retain, upsell, acquire."
4. **Multi-store / brand portfolio:** operators who run several Shopify brands.

## Plays

1. **Earn the upsell with proof.** Only expand after the weekly recap shows real lift. "Email
   is producing $X/mo. Adding SMS would add ~$Y. Want me to build it?" (Aria voice.)
2. **Trigger-based offers, not calendar blasts.** Upsell when a usage/result trigger fires
   (hit volume cap, list grew, a channel maxed out), not on a fixed schedule.
3. **Make expansion one-click.** Aria proposes, customer approves — same approval pattern as
   flows. Low friction = high attach.
4. **Cross-sell the roadmap.** As new capabilities ship (SMS, analytics, acquisition), Aria
   proactively offers them to fitting accounts with an expected-revenue estimate.
5. **Annual / commitment offers** for proven-happy customers (improves cash + retention).

## Assets Claude can generate
- Upsell proposal template (proof → projected lift → one-click approve), Aria voice
- Trigger map: which usage/result signals fire which expansion offer
- New-capability cross-sell announcement (per ICP segment)
- Expansion-revenue forecast model

## What to log back here
- NRR by cohort; expansion MRR by path (tier vs. channel vs. capability)
- Which triggers convert to expansion best
- Attach rate of each new capability
- Expansion CAC (usually ~$0 — quantify the leverage)
