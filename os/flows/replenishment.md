# Replenishment / Reorder Flow — turn one-time buyers into repeat revenue

**Trigger:** predicted run-out — fires a few days before the customer is likely to finish the
product (based on category + their purchase history). **Goal:** automatic repeat purchases.
**The biggest lever for F&B / supplements / consumable beauty** — the highest-frequency, highest-
repurchase ICP brands where email prints money. **Metric:** reorder rate + revenue per recipient.

## Timing (fire BEFORE they run out)
Estimate the consumption cycle from the product + their order history; send at ~80–90% of the cycle.
- Supplements: ~25–28 days (30-day supply) · Coffee/consumables: ~21–30d · Skincare: ~30–45d.

## Structure (2–3 emails)
| # | Send | Job | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | day ~25 (pre-run-out) | "time to restock" — one-click reorder | none / subscribe-and-save |
| 2 | +4 days | nudge + convenience (subscribe) | small incentive or S&S discount |
| 3 (opt) | +7 days | last nudge | best incentive |

---

### Email 1 — time to restock (pre-run-out)
**Subjects:** (1) running low on [product]? · (2) time to restock, [First name] · (3) don't run out
**Body:**
> Hey [First name],
> By our math, you're about to run out of [product] — want us to get the next one on its way?
> **[Reorder in one click →]**
> Prefer never to think about it? **Subscribe & save [X]%** — delivered on your schedule.

### Email 2 — convenience + save (+4 days)
**Subjects:** (1) never run out again · (2) save [X]% on your refill · (3) your [product], on autopilot
**Body:**
> Restock the easy way, [First name]: **Subscribe & Save [X]%** — same [product], delivered every
> [N] weeks, cancel anytime.
> **[Set it and forget it →]**  ·  or  **[Just reorder once →]**

### Email 3 (optional) — last nudge (+7 days)
**Subjects:** (1) before you run out · (2) your refill's waiting · (3) last reminder
**Body:**
> Don't get caught empty, [First name] — your [product] is one click away.
> [If approved: here's [X]% to restock today: **code**.]
> **[Reorder now →]**

---

## Category adaptations
- **Supplements/F&B:** lean hard into subscribe-and-save; it's the expansion path (→ `expansion-upsell-engine.md`).
- **Beauty (consumable):** routine reminder ("your [serum] is about due") + pair with a complement (cross-sell).
- **Kids consumables (diapers, wipes, formula):** size-up + restock framing for parents.

## Connects to
- **Expansion:** subscribe-and-save conversions lift NRR + blended ACV.
- **Win-Back:** if they lapse past the cycle, the Win-Back flow picks them up.

## A/B test & log
Test: send timing (% of cycle), one-time reorder vs subscribe-first, incentive depth. Log reorder
rate + S&S conversion + revenue/recipient + best timing → README + CS playbook.
