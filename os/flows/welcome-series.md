# Welcome Series — turn new subscribers into first orders

**Trigger:** new subscriber (popup, checkout opt-in, list join). **Goal:** convert to first order +
set the brand relationship. **Why it matters most for high-ad-spend brands:** you're paying to fill
the list — this is what monetizes it. **Metric:** first-order rate + revenue per new subscriber.

## Structure (3–4 emails)
| # | Send | Job | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | immediate | welcome + deliver the opt-in promise (e.g., 10% code) | the signup offer |
| 2 | +2 days | brand story + why it's different | reminder of code |
| 3 | +4 days | best-sellers / social proof | code expiring |
| 4 (opt) | +6 days | category guide / quiz / final nudge | last call |

---

### Email 1 — welcome (immediate)
**Subjects:** (1) welcome to [Brand] — here's your 10% · (2) you're in, [First name] · (3) your code's inside
**Body:**
> Welcome, [First name] 👋
> Here's the **10% off** we promised: **[code]**.
> [Brand] is [one-line positioning — what we make + who for].
> Start here: **[Shop best-sellers →]**

### Email 2 — the story (+2 days)
**Subjects:** (1) why we started [Brand] · (2) what makes [product] different · (3) the [Brand] difference
**Body:**
> Quick story, [First name] —
> [1–2 lines: founder/why/what makes the product different — the real brand story].
> Your 10% code **[code]** is still good.
> **[See what we're about →]**

### Email 3 — proof + best-sellers (+4 days)
**Subjects:** (1) [N] customers love these · (2) our most-loved, [First name] · (3) your 10% expires soon
**Body:**
> Not sure where to start? These are our most-loved:
> • [Best-seller 1] — [one-line] • [Best-seller 2] — [one-line]
> Rated [4.8★] by [N] customers. *(real)* Your **10%** expires [date].
> **[Shop best-sellers →]**

### Email 4 (optional) — final nudge (+6 days)
**Subjects:** (1) last call on your welcome offer · (2) before your code expires · (3) one more thing
**Body:**
> Last reminder, [First name] — your welcome **10%** expires tonight: **[code]**.
> **[Use it before it's gone →]**

---

## Category adaptations
- **Beauty:** add a quick routine/shade finder; recommend by skin/hair type.
- **F&B/supplements:** how-to-use + a subscribe-and-save intro.
- **Apparel:** size guide + the current drop/season.
- **Kids:** by-age recommendations for parents.
- **Community-led:** open in the community's voice/language (ICP lanes).

## A/B test & log
Test: offer (% vs $ vs free ship), 3 vs 4 emails, story-first vs product-first. Log first-order
rate + revenue/new-subscriber + winning subject → README + CS playbook.
