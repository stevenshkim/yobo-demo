# Abandoned Cart Flow — recover what you lose nightly

**Trigger:** cart created / checkout started, no purchase. **Goal:** recover abandoned revenue.
**Common gap:** most brands run *one* email here — it should be three.
**Metric:** recovery rate + revenue per recipient. **Voice:** Aria. Merchant approves every send.

## Structure (3 emails)
| # | Send | Job | Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | +1 hour | helpful nudge — "you left something" | none |
| 2 | +24 hours | handle hesitation + proof | none / soft |
| 3 | +48 hours | urgency + (if needed) incentive | light incentive |

> Keep email 1 incentive-free — many recover without a discount. Don't train customers to abandon.

---

### Email 1 — the nudge (+1 hour)
**Subjects:** (1) you left something behind · (2) still want [product]? · (3) your cart's waiting
**Body:**
> Hey [First name],
> Looks like you left [product] in your cart. We saved it for you.
> [One-line benefit / why it's worth it].
> **[Finish checking out →]**

### Email 2 — handle hesitation (+24 hours)
**Subjects:** (1) questions about [product]? · (2) [4.8★] — here's what people say · (3) still thinking it over?
**Body:**
> Still deciding, [First name]?
> [Product] is rated [4.8★] by [N] customers — [one-line review]. *(real review only)*
> Free shipping over $[X] · easy returns. Anything we can answer?
> **[Complete your order →]**

### Email 3 — urgency / last nudge (+48 hours)
**Subjects:** (1) your cart's about to expire · (2) last call on [product] · (3) before it's gone
**Body:**
> Last reminder, [First name] — [product] is still in your cart but may sell out / your cart expires soon.
> [If approved: Here's 10% to finish: **code**.]
> **[Check out now →]**

---

## Category adaptations
- **Apparel:** show the item image + size/color they chose; "back in your size while it lasts."
- **Beauty/F&B:** bundle/regimen nudge ("complete your routine"); free-shipping threshold push.
- **High-AOV:** lead with risk-reversal (returns, guarantee) over discount.

## A/B test & log
Test: delays (1h vs 2h), incentive in email 3 vs none, urgency framing. Log recovery rate +
revenue/recipient + winning subject → README + CS playbook.
