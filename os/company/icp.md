# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

> Load this before any lead-gen, outbound, ad, or positioning work. Targeting is the
> cheapest lever on CAC — the right list beats clever copy.

## Who we sell to

**US-based, Shopify-powered, direct-to-consumer brands with enough email-addressable
revenue that lifecycle marketing moves the needle — but not so large they already have a
mature in-house retention team.**

### Firmographic filter (the qualified-list definition)

| Attribute | Sweet spot | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Platform | Shopify / Shopify Plus | Our integration is native here |
| Email tool | Klaviyo (installed) | We plug straight in; shortest time-to-value |
| Geography | United States | Goal is US customers; aligns time zones + compliance |
| Annual revenue | ~$1M–$20M | Big enough to afford $500/mo + value retention; small enough to lack an in-house lifecycle team |
| Monthly orders | 1,000+ | Enough volume for flows to produce measurable lift |
| List size | 10k–500k contacts | Material email-addressable base |
| Team | No dedicated retention/email hire | We *are* that hire |

### Anti-ICP (do not pursue — they wreck CAC and churn)
- Pre-revenue / < $250k ARR brands (can't afford, won't retain)
- Non-Shopify or non-Klaviyo stacks (no fast time-to-value yet)
- Enterprise with in-house lifecycle teams (long cycle, custom, off-motion)
- Marketplaces/resellers without a direct customer email relationship

## The buyer

- **Title:** Founder/CEO (at $1–5M), or Head of Growth / Ecommerce / Retention (at $5–20M).
- **Reports to / cares about:** revenue, repeat-purchase rate, CAC payback, "I'm leaving
  money on the table in email."
- **Day-to-day pain:** email is an afterthought, flows are stale or unbuilt, no time/talent,
  agencies are slow and expensive, Klaviyo is powerful but unused.

## Buying triggers (when to reach out — these spike conversion)

- Just crossed a revenue/volume threshold (hiring growth roles)
- Klaviyo installed but flows empty/stale (detectable signal)
- Recently raised, or scaling ad spend (acquisition up, retention neglected)
- Seasonal pressure (BFCM, product launch, holiday) approaching
- A retention/email hire just left (gap to fill)

## The core pains we remove (use these as messaging hooks)

1. **"I know I'm leaving money in email but don't have time/talent."** → Aria runs it.
2. **"Agencies are slow, expensive, and generic."** → AI-native, fast, on-brand, guaranteed.
3. **"Klaviyo is a powerful tool I barely use."** → We turn it into revenue, not a checkbox.
4. **"Retention is an afterthought vs. paid acquisition."** → We make repeat revenue a system.

## How ICP maps to the funnels
- **Lead gen:** the firmographic filter + triggers define the list and the ad audiences.
- **Activation:** ICP fit predicts who hits "aha" fast — prioritize them in trial.
- **Customer success:** off-ICP customers churn; weight onboarding effort to in-ICP.
- **Expansion:** in-ICP brands have the most channels to expand into (SMS, more flows).
