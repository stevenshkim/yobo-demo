# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Segmentation

> Load before any lead-gen, outbound, ad, content, or positioning work. Targeting is the
> cheapest lever on CAC — the right list beats clever copy. This model also predicts **who will
> succeed** (and safely hit the 3× guarantee) and **what content will go viral** in which community.

## Who we sell to (the frame)

**US-based, Shopify-powered, Klaviyo-using DTC brands above the store-size floor** — big enough
that lifecycle email moves real money, not so big they have a mature in-house retention team.
**Store size is read straight from Shopify on sync** (revenue, orders), so qualification is
automatic, not guesswork.

**Store-size floor (also the guarantee floor):** ≥ **$500K/yr** OR ≥ **$42K/mo** OR ≥ **1,000
orders/mo**. Below this, email can't realistically return 3× — so they don't qualify.

## The fit-and-success scorecard (9 dimensions)

Every prospect/customer is read on these nine. They do **two jobs**: predict **success +
guarantee safety** (will email produce results?) and predict **virality** (will community
content spread?). Score each Ideal / OK / Avoid.

| # | Dimension | Ideal | OK | Avoid | Read from | Primary job |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Founder origin / community** | A defined community (see clusters below) | mixed | none / irrelevant to brand | public / brand | Virality + content match |
| 2 | **Customer community / language / origin** | concentrated community + language | broad US | undefined | Shopify (geo, language), reviews | Virality + content match |
| 3 | **Category** | Beauty, F&B/supplements, Kids | Fashion, Home, Sports | one-time/durable, low-repeat | Shopify products | Wedge flow + angle |
| 4 | **Revenue / transaction size** | $1–20M (above floor) | $500K–$1M | below floor | **Shopify on sync** | Qualification + tier + guarantee |
| 5 | **Purchase frequency / yr** | **6+** (replenishment) | **2+** (power users) | 1 / one-time | Shopify orders | Success + guarantee safety |
| 6 | **AOV / avg SKU price** | healthy margin to support flows | low but high-frequency | tiny AOV + low frequency | Shopify | Success + offer design |
| 7 | **Reviews** | **4+ / 5** | 3.5–4 | < 3.5 (quality issues) | Shopify/Judge.me/public | Success + retention + low refund |
| 8 | **Repurchase rate** | high | moderate | near-zero | Shopify/Klaviyo | Success — lifecycle leverage |
| 9 | **Ad spend (lead-gen + conversion)** | actively spending | some | none / no growth intent | public (Meta Ad Library), signals | Can afford us + retargeting pools + growth mindset |

> **A brand that's high on 5–8 is where lifecycle email prints money** — they'll succeed, retain,
> and we'll safely clear the 3× guarantee. A brand that's strong on 1–3 is where **community
> content goes viral.** The best targets score high on both halves.

## The success predictor (who hits 3× and retains) — dims 4–8

These tell us, *before* we spend a dollar acquiring them, whether email will work for them —
which protects CAC, retention, and the guarantee:

- **Frequency 6+/yr** = replenishment behavior → Win-Back, replenishment, and reorder flows
  print revenue. **2+/yr power users** also work (bigger baskets, win-back-able).
- **Repurchase rate high** = there's a returning-customer base for lifecycle email to harvest.
- **Reviews 4+/5** = product quality is real → low refunds, high trust, email converts and
  doesn't get killed by dissatisfaction.
- **Healthy AOV** = each recovered order is worth chasing; supports the flow economics.

**Use it for:** lead-gen targeting (prioritize high-success-score accounts), activation
(predict who hits "aha" fast), customer success (off-predictor customers churn), and **guarantee
safety** (these brands clear 3× of fees easily — `os/company/guarantee-mechanics.md`).

## The virality / community lens (the content engine's targeting) — dims 1–3

**Insight (learned):** community/diaspora-led brands grow virally *within* their community, and
content that matches the audience's **language + culture + category** spreads far cheaper than
generic content. This is the targeting brain for the **Tier-2 AI educational accounts / AI
influencers** in the build plan (`os/gtm-build-plan.md` C3) — and a strong customer-success
signal (community brands have loyal, high-repurchase audiences).

> Used as an **affinity / community-resonance and creator-matching** lens — for cultural
> relevance and content fit, applied respectfully. Not exclusionary targeting.

**Community clusters (founder &/or customer), for content + creator matching:**
Latino (Spanish — Mexican, etc.) · Indian · Chinese · Korean · Brazilian · Native-English white
(American, Australian, British, Canadian, etc.) · American Black · African · European (Italian,
French, Greek, Turkish, German, Russian, etc.) · Middle Eastern.

**How it drives the content engine:**
- **Category × community = a content "lane."** e.g., *Latina beauty*, *Korean skincare*,
  *Indian F&B*, *African-American haircare*.
- Each lane gets **matched AI educational accounts / AI influencers** producing content in the
  community's language + cultural context → resonates, spreads, drives audits at near-zero CAC.
- The same lens personalizes outreach + ad creative (language, cultural cues, community proof).

## Target-account list criteria (actionable — feeds outreach, paid, partnerships)

Build the target list with these filters (source/enrich per signal):

1. **Platform:** Shopify / Shopify Plus + **Klaviyo installed** (BuiltWith/Store Leads/wappalyzer).
2. **Geography:** United States.
3. **Store size:** above the floor (revenue/orders — confirmed on Shopify sync; pre-sync, estimate
   from traffic/order signals).
4. **Category:** Beauty · F&B/supplements · Kids · Fashion · Home · Sports · Digital.
5. **Success signals:** high review count at 4+/5; visible repeat/subscription offers; replenishable
   products (frequency proxy).
6. **Ad-spend signal:** active in **Meta Ad Library** / running Google Shopping (growth intent + retargeting pools).
7. **Community signal:** identifiable founder/customer community (for lane matching).
8. **Buying triggers (prioritize):** Klaviyo installed but flows stale/empty · recently raised ·
   scaling ad spend · seasonal pressure (BFCM/launch) · lost a retention/email hire.

## Anti-ICP (do not pursue — they wreck CAC, churn, and the guarantee)

- Below the store-size floor (can't hit 3×, can't afford, won't retain).
- **One-time / low-frequency, low-repurchase** products (durables, single-purchase) — email can't compound.
- **Reviews < 3.5 / quality problems** — email amplifies dissatisfaction; refunds + churn.
- Non-Shopify or non-Klaviyo stacks (no fast time-to-value yet).
- Enterprise with in-house lifecycle teams (long cycle, off-motion).
- No ad spend + no growth intent (won't value or scale with us).

## How ICP maps to the funnels

- **Lead gen:** the list criteria + triggers define lists + ad audiences; community lens defines content lanes.
- **Activation:** success-predictor (freq/repurchase/reviews/AOV) flags who hits "aha" fast — prioritize them.
- **Customer success:** off-predictor customers churn; weight onboarding to high-score, in-ICP brands.
- **Revenue expansion:** high-frequency, multi-category brands have the most channels to expand into.
- **Guarantee safety:** the floor + success-predictor keep the 3× guarantee profitable.
