# Offer & Positioning

> What we sell, how it's priced, the promise, and the messaging spine. Load before any
> landing page, ad, sales, or pricing work.

## One-liner

**YoboLabs is the AI-native growth marketer for consumer brands.** Today we run your
Klaviyo email marketing end-to-end — curate the flows, build the creative, ship to Klaviyo,
monitor, and improve — so you grow repeat revenue without hiring a team or babysitting an
agency. Tomorrow, the same agent acquires, retains, and expands across every channel.

## The product (what the customer actually gets)

- **Aria** — a growth-manager agent who works like a senior hire, not a tool. You chat;
  she plans, builds, ships, monitors, iterates.
- **Connect Shopify + Klaviyo** → she analyzes your store + list and proposes a real
  lifecycle plan (goals, segmentation, offer design, channels).
- **Campaign + flow generation** — Win-Back, Abandoned Cart, Welcome Series, etc., with
  on-brand creative and copy, pushed to Klaviyo on approval.
- **Always-on** — daily updates, weekly revenue recaps, monthly plan proposals.
- **You stay in control** — approval modes from manual review to full autopilot.

**Live today:** Shopify + Klaviyo integration, plan generation, campaign generation (goals,
segmentation, offer design, channels). **Refining:** UX + email creation.
**Roadmap:** SMS/WhatsApp, retention analytics, upsell/cross-sell, paid acquisition assist —
the full "AI growth marketer."

**Category:** not "an email tool" — **an AI growth marketer you hire.** Frame as a *team
member*, priced like software.

## The promise / guarantee

**"3× revenue guaranteed"** (from yobolabs.com positioning). Powerful, but only usable where
the mechanics are defined and Legal-cleared:
- **3× of what, over what window, attributed how?** (e.g., 3× the Aria-attributed email
  revenue vs. prior baseline within 90 days.)
- **Remedy if not met** (credit, free months, refund?).
- **Eligibility** (must approve X flows, connect properly, hit minimum send volume).
