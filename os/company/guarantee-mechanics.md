# The 3× Guarantee — Mechanics (DRAFT for Legal)

> **STATUS: DRAFT — NOT LEGALLY CLEARED. Do not use the "3× guaranteed" claim in ads,
> landing pages, or contracts until Legal signs off on this page.** Until then, use softer,
> real-data proof ("brands typically see X% lift") per `os/company/offer-and-positioning.md`.

The "3× revenue guaranteed" line is powerful but only usable once it answers four questions
precisely: **3× of what, measured how, over what window, with what remedy — and who qualifies.**
This page proposes the mechanics so Legal can review and finalize.

---

## 1. The claim, stated precisely (proposed)

> "Within 90 days of your first flow going live, the email revenue Aria drives will reach at
> least **3× your pre-YoboLabs email-attributed revenue baseline** — or we keep working for
> free until it does."

## 2. "3× of what" — the baseline

- **Baseline = the brand's trailing email-attributed revenue** over the 90 days *before* their
  first Aria-built flow goes live, pulled from Klaviyo (their existing flow/campaign revenue).
- If the brand had **no flows / ~$0 email revenue** before, set a floor baseline so "3×" is
  meaningful (e.g., measure against an absolute target agreed at onboarding, not 3×$0).
- Recorded and shown to the customer at onboarding so the number is transparent and mutual.

## 3. "Measured how" — attribution

- **Metric = Aria-attributed email revenue** = Klaviyo-attributed revenue (Klaviyo's default
  attribution window) from flows and campaigns Aria built or rebuilt.
- Reported monthly in the weekly/monthly recap so the customer always knows their multiple
  ("you're at 2.1× — here's the plan to 3×").
- **Decision for Legal/Steven:** use Klaviyo last-click attributed revenue (simplest, the
  customer already sees it in Klaviyo) vs. incremental-vs-baseline. Recommend **Klaviyo
  attributed** for transparency and low dispute risk.

## 4. "Over what window"

- **90 days from first flow live** (not from signup — protects against slow onboarders).
- Re-evaluated at day 90; progress shown continuously before then.

## 5. The remedy (pick one — recommend A)

| Option | Mechanic | Cash risk | Strength of claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Keep-working-free** (recommend) | If < 3× at day 90, we waive monthly fees and keep building until 3× is hit | Low (no refund; we just extend service) | Strong, confident, low dispute |
| B. Fee refund | Refund fees paid during the 90 days | High (direct cash out) | Strong but expensive |
| C. Credit | Credit equal to fees toward future months | Medium | Weaker (locks them in, can feel cheap) |

**A** is recommended: it signals total confidence, costs only marginal service time (not cash),
and keeps the relationship going — which is itself retention.

## 6. Eligibility (the conditions that make it fair + defensible)

The guarantee applies only if the customer:
1. Connects Shopify + Klaviyo with the required scopes within 7 days of starting paid.
2. **Approves at least [N] flows** Aria proposes within [14] days (we can't deliver if nothing ships).
3. Keeps approved flows **live and sending** (doesn't pause/disable them).
4. Maintains their list (no mass deletion / import of cold lists that wreck deliverability).
5. Meets a **minimum list size / send volume** (below it, email can't produce 3× — define the floor).
6. Is on a qualifying paid tier (define: all tiers, or Senior+?).

## 7. Exclusions (out of our control)

- Deliverability collapse caused by the customer (spam complaints, buying lists, domain issues).
- Major store disruptions (going out of stock, site down, pausing the business).
- Seasonality games (comparing a peak baseline to an off-season window) — define windows fairly.
- Force majeure / platform outages (Klaviyo or Shopify downtime).

## 8. What Legal must clear before we publish the claim

- **Substantiation (FTC):** an objective, documented basis for "3×"; keep the per-customer
  baseline + attribution records on file.
- The word **"guaranteed"** and the remedy must appear in the Terms of Service / order form.
- Refund/credit terms (if Option B/C) comply with billing + consumer-protection rules.
- Clear, accessible disclosure of the eligibility conditions wherever the claim appears.
- CASL/state nuances if offered to any non-US customers.
